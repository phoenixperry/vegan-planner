Lift · fuel · your plan

Daily targets

~1,600 kcal≥130g protein≥1,000mg Cafat ≥50gmaint ~1,900~0.3 kg/wk
  • 62.6 → ~57.6 kg over ~4 months. Slow is the point — it keeps the loss fat, not muscle or bone.
  • Protein at every meal (~35–40 g) is the biggest lever for holding muscle in a deficit.
  • Put most of your carbs around your 3 gym sessions.

Floors — stop here, not lower

  • End the cut at ~57–58 kg or ~18% body fat, whichever comes first.
  • Never below ~1,500 kcal or ~50 g fat in a day.
  • Diet break: eat at maintenance (~1,900) for a week every 6–8 weeks.
  • Ease off if energy, sleep, strength or your cycle slip — that's the plan working, not failing.
Training · your week

The shape of it

  • 2 × EGYM circuit with your friend (below) — your main muscle work.
  • 1 × bone day of free weights — the heavy loading machines can't give your skeleton.
  • Space them ~48 h apart; 8–10k steps on the other days.

EGYM circuit — with your friend ×2 / wk

  • 4 stations, 2 machines each. You each take one, do your set, then swap — nobody waits.
  • 8–12 hard reps, muscle-building goal, eccentric mode on the presses & pulls. 2–3 rounds.
  • Re-run the strength test and pick muscle-building — you've been in the 15–21 "toning" zone, so expect heavier than the reference loads below.
  • Machine busy? Skip to the next station and loop back — EGYM holds your place.
  • Spine-safe: your seated machines keep your back supported — ideal for you. Station 4 swaps the twisting rotary torso for an anti-rotation Pallof press, and don't let your low back round at the bottom of the leg press.

Bone day — free weights ×1 / wk

  • Seated machines can't load your skeleton — this is the day that builds your spine. Load it through a neutral (not rounded) spine; the trap-bar makes that easier. Solo, or bring your friend.

Loaded Carry (farmer's)3 × ~30 m
  • Deadlift: heavy triples, 1 kg microplates, straps so grip isn't the limiter. Keep every rep a neutral spine.
  • Heavy lifting like this is proven to raise spine density even at low bone mass (the LIFTMOR trial) — so progress the load; the old "don't lift" fear had it backwards. A few sessions with a coach or bone-health physio to groove the form are worth it.

Progress & recover

  • Double progression: clear the top rep on every set → EGYM bumps you next round (add 2.5 kg upper / 5 kg lower on free weights).
  • Deload week 6: 2 sets/exercise at RPE 6, same weights.
  • 8–10k steps/day is your main fat-loss driver; keep heavy HIIT out of the deficit.
  • Sleep 7–9 h — the biggest lever for holding muscle.

Supplements

  • Creatine 5 g/day — the biggest single win on a vegan diet.
  • B12 250 mcg/day · Vitamin D3 (vegan) 1,000–2,000 IU/day.
  • Algae omega-3 (EPA/DHA) 250–500 mg/day.
  • Calcium top-up to 1,000–1,200 mg if food falls short · K2 (MK-7) optional for bone.
Watch for under-fuelling. Lingering fatigue, sliding strength, poor sleep, feeling cold, low mood, cycle changes or frequent niggles → eat at maintenance for a week. Given a long vegan cut at 51, a GP blood panel (ferritin, vitamin D, B12, thyroid) and a DEXA baseline are worth asking for.
